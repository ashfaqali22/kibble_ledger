Extrusion is why kibble is shelf-stable without refrigeration: cooking under pressure gelatinizes starches and largely sterilizes the mix, and drying to low moisture removes the water activity that spoilage organisms need. The coating stage — fats and palatants applied hot, then tumbled — is what makes a nutritionally complete formula something an animal will actually eat on day one.

Wet food

Cans, pouches, trays. ~75–80% moisture, shelf-stable via retort (thermal) sterilization while sealed, short shelf life once opened. Higher cost per kg of actual nutrition, often used for palatability and hydration, and to signal premium positioning.

Treats & snacks

Small volume, high margin, impulse-purchase. Functionally a bonding ritual between owner and pet as much as nutrition — often the lowest-risk entry point for a consumer to trial a new brand.

Complete vs. complementary

"Complete" food must meet a full nutrient-adequacy profile as a sole diet. "Complementary" food (most treats, some wet formats) is not nutritionally sufficient alone and must be fed alongside a complete diet.

03

How Pet Food Quality Actually Works

What determines quality beneath the marketing claim — and how an owner actually judges it, which is rarely the same thing.

Protein — source beats headline %

A named animal-meal protein with a strong amino acid profile and high digestibility outperforms a higher "crude protein %" built from cheap plant protein that inflates the label without being as bioavailable.

Fat

Drives energy density and palatability, and carries essential fatty acids — but is also the main oxidation/rancidity risk over time, especially in a hot, humid climate.

Carbohydrate, fibre, ash

Carbs bind the kibble structurally during extrusion; fibre supports stool quality; ash (mineral residue) matters directly for urinary health, particularly in cats.

Moisture control

The single biggest shelf-stability lever. Bangladesh's ambient humidity (Dhaka averages 72%, up to 81% in July Verified) already exceeds safe passive-storage thresholds — this is a real operating constraint, not a theoretical one.

Digestibility & palatability

Higher digestibility means less stool volume and better nutrient uptake. Palatants determine the very first hurdle: whether the animal eats it at all.

Oxidation & rancidity

Fat oxidation degrades both nutrition and smell over time. Mitigated by antioxidants, barrier packaging and strict FIFO inventory rotation — not by formulation alone.

Nutritional science rarely enters this chain directly — it shows up indirectly, through stool and coat, weeks after purchase. This is why trial packs and consistent quality matter more commercially than a nutrition panel most owners will never read closely.

International standards vs. Bangladesh law: AAFCO (United States) and FEDIAF (Europe) publish nutrient-adequacy profiles that function as de facto global quality benchmarks. Bangladesh has no published pet-food-specific nutrient standard — the Animal Feed Rules 2013 govern feed broadly, not pet-food nutrient adequacy specifically. Adopting AAFCO/FEDIAF profiles would be an AKG internal standard, not a Bangladesh legal requirement. This distinction is carried through consistently in Chapter 22.

Sampling & trial packs

Because the animal's approval is a hard gate the owner can't fully predict in advance, small trial formats de-risk the FIRST purchase far more than they would in a human-only category.

Repeat measurement

"Repeat" has to be watched separately from "trial" — the awareness-to-use gap in Chapter 5 shows plenty of category interest stalls before it ever converts once, let alone repeats.

The 5,000 MT Scale Reference

One number, three meanings — and they are not interchangeable.

Cumulative sales volume

Product actually sold over a defined period. A historical fact, measured after it happens.

Annualized run-rate

What the business would sell over 12 months if the current monthly pace continued. A projection from a point in time, not a promise.

Packaging / operating capacity

What the physical operation can safely and practically handle. An engineering ceiling, independent of what is actually sold.

5,000 MT is a commercial scale reference Management ambition — the maximum figure used anywhere in this document's scenario planning. It is not a research forecast, not a guaranteed sales result, not a Year-1 commitment, and not a market-volume-share calculation (Chapter 4 explains why volume share cannot currently be calculated at all). It is the ceiling the commercial and operating architecture is designed around.
