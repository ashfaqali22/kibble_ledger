Supply

Two Qualified Manufacturers Secure the Year-1 Requirement

Primary

Hebei Shengxi Pet Food Co., Ltd.

  • Halal-certified, plus HACCP, FSSC 22000, ISO, US FDA registration
  • >50,000 MT/year capacity — well beyond the required run-rate
  • Dry-kibble bulk-order capability explicitly confirmed
  • Full formula, shape, packaging and branding customization
Backup

Gambol Pet Group

  • Broadest certification set reviewed — exports to 30+ countries
  • Group scale: 25 factories, ~4,800 SKUs produced per year
  • Operates a plant in Thailand — a hedge against origin concentration
  • Three in-house R&D centres for formulation support
Both are private-label manufacturers; client brands are not publicly disclosed by either, which is standard practice at this tier. Commercial terms — price, minimum order, lead time — are not yet confirmed with either party.

What Must Be Built

Scale Requires an Industrial Supply Chain From Month One

Supply

  • 5,340 MT/yr, peaking at 1,200 MT/month
  • Two qualified manufacturers, each capable of full run-rate
  • Shipment cadence: weekly to fortnightly at peak

Repack

  • ~48 MT/day peak throughput required
  • Buffer sized at 1.3–1.5× peak for seasonality
  • Industrial-scale line — not yet costed at this size

Distribution

  • National distributor live by Month 7–9
  • Specialist, modern trade and e-commerce, nationally
  • Dhaka/Chattogram → 3 cities → national in 12 months

Quality

  • Batch traceability, source to consumer, at national scale
  • A quality incident reaches a national footprint on day one
  • Recall readiness built in from launch
